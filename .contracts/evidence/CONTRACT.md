# Evidence

## Rules

- Keep lifecycle independent of assessment. Support qualitative work without metrics.
- Preserve omitted patch fields and retain every successful record revision.
- Pin derived_from links to saved target revisions. Keep ancestry acyclic while allowing ordinary evidence cycles.
- Preserve measurements; reject conflicting values at the same record, metric, cohort, and step.
- Compare matching metric/cohort/unit/direction only. Disclose chart truncation.
- Attribute findings and retain supporting/contradicting links. Actor names are caller-supplied attribution.
- Label synthetic examples. Never present them as empirical policy performance.
- Treat artifacts as references until byte ingestion and verification exist.

## Checks

Store tests check evidence preservation and conflicting evidence retrieval. Inspect comparisons across different cohorts.
Read a finding against its sources: does the claim exceed the observations?
