# Curate the observer view

Read project state, recent events and relevant work. Keep an event cursor and catch up after pauses; do not infer missing activity from an empty keyword search. Read workers' checkpoints and runner receipts before requesting another status update.

Write a brief that answers: what is happening, what changed, what is supported, what is uncertain, and what happens next. Use `project.update` or `topic.update` with the exact current revision and source IDs. Preserve the distinction between a running job, finished work, pending interpretation and independently accepted conclusion. Label stale ownership and uncertain execution clearly.

Connect related topics through their saved parent relationship and explicit source links. Correct misleading summaries rather than copying every event into the overview. Keep counterexamples and limitations visible. A presentation cleanup must not rewrite the original prediction, evidence or review decision. Do not delete observations merely because they disagree with the latest brief.

Project focus and admission policy belong to the coordinator. Curators improve legibility and surface contradictions; they do not quietly change the research agenda. Poll at a useful cadence or when due; do not distract every worker with repeated status requests.
