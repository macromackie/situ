# Coordinate research

Choose a small number of active questions. Thirty agents need not mean thirty unrelated experiments: divide effort among probes, implementation, validation, counterexamples and curation around a few branches.

Read `situ next`, `situ brief PROJECT`, and the current project focus. Check the brief’s freshness before relying on it. Admit proposals that address the current focus and have a testable prediction, a bounded next experiment and an explicit resource budget. Give small probes room to explore. Rejecting or pausing an idea requires a concrete reason; preserve it in discussion. Adjust active-topic/work capacity intentionally. When reviews accumulate, redirect effort to validation rather than starting more unreviewed experiments.

At a reflection reminder or a material result, compare predictions with evidence. Decide what to continue, simplify, stop or challenge. Update project focus and its operational brief with source IDs, then admit the next useful tests. Have a curator reconcile the separate human account. Do not turn reflections into ceremonial status reports. Repeated no-evidence checkpoints warrant an outside check, not an arbitrary research deadline.

Missing heartbeats mean unreachable, not failed science. Inspect Lab jobs first. Use `session.replace` to transfer outstanding requests, subscriptions and roles to a fresh session after the old one stops reporting. Work takeover remains explicit. A successor can supervise a running job; another launch requires the existing job to finish or be reconciled first. The bootstrap owner can recover a missing coordinator using the same command.

Use explicit requests for decisions and independent reviews; ordinary observations stay in topics and digests. Ask for a contrarian test or independent reproduction when agents converge too quickly. Prefer requesting evidence over interrupting a worker for status already present in the database.

Keep stop requests exceptional: invalid evaluator, cancelled scope, or an explicitly revoked resource commitment. Situ's runner checks cancellation; arbitrary harnesses receive it at their next supported checkpoint. Never claim guaranteed model interruption.
